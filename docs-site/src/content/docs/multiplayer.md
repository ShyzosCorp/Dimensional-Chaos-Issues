Use the same pack release as the server. Teams use Slate Teams, which the modpack includes. A custom installation needs it too.

## Create a team

Open the Slate Teams menu (**K** by default) and choose **Create a team**. Pick a name, then **Found team**. Your island and items stay with you, and your island becomes the team island.

`/dc team create <name>` does the same from chat. `/dc team info` shows your team and island.

## Invite and join

Invite players from the team menu with **Invite player**. Slate's `/team invite <player>` also works. An invitation stays open for a limited time.

The invited player accepts in their own team menu, or with the **[Accept]** link in chat.

**Accepting an invitation joins the team straight away and resets your carried inventory, accessories, Ender Chest, XP and unrecovered graves once.** Storage blocks you placed stay where they are. Empty your inventory into a chest and finish any expedition before you accept.

Players can also ask to join from the team list. A request never adds anyone directly: a member who can invite approves it, which turns it into an invitation. If the player is online, they join right away, with the same reset. Requests expire after 30 minutes. Invite-only teams don't take requests.

## What the team shares

Team members share an island, OneBlock progress, its six-slot reserve and the island expansion tier. Quest progress and team rewards follow the pack's Slate quest settings.

## Leave, remove or hand over

Use the team menu to leave, remove a member, hand over ownership or disband. Slate asks you to confirm. Items, storage, XP and graves are kept. A player who leaves or is removed goes back to their own island. The team island stays with the team, and the founder gets it back if the team is disbanded.

The same actions exist as commands: `/dc team leave`, `/dc team kick <member>`, `/dc team transfer <member>` and `/dc team disband`. Each asks you to confirm within 60 seconds.

If a team change is refused, read the message and ask the server owner for help. Don't work around it by editing player data or deleting transfer files.
