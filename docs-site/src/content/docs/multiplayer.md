Use the same pack version as the server. Teams use Slate Teams, which the modpack includes.

## Official server

Want to play with others? Join the official server at **play-dc.shyzos.xyz**. In the modpack, it's listed under **Community** on the multiplayer screen.

## Create a team

Open the Slate Teams menu (**K** by default) and choose **Create a team**. Pick a name, then **Found team**. You keep your island and items, and your island becomes the team's island. This works whether you're an operator or not.

`/dc team create <name>` does the same from chat, and `/dc team info` shows your team and island.

## Invite and join

Invite players from the team menu with **Invite player**, or with Slate's `/team invite <player>`. Invitations expire after a while.

The invited player accepts from their own team menu, or with the **[Accept]** link in chat.

**Accepting an invitation joins the team right away and resets your inventory, accessories, Ender Chest, XP and unrecovered graves, once.** Blocks and chests you placed stay where they are. Put your stuff in a chest and finish any expedition before you accept.

Players can also ask to join from the team list. A request never adds anyone by itself: a member who can invite approves it, which turns it into an invitation. If the player is online, they join right away, with the same reset. Requests expire after 30 minutes, and invite-only teams don't take them.

## What the team shares

Team members share the island, the OneBlock and its six-slot reserve, and the island tier. Quest progress and rewards follow the pack's Slate quest settings. Each player has their own expedition timer.

## Leave, remove or hand over

Use the team menu to leave, remove a member, hand over ownership or disband. Slate asks you to confirm. Items, storage, XP and graves are kept. A player who leaves or is removed goes back to their own island. The team island stays with the team, and the founder gets it back if the team is disbanded.

The same actions exist as commands: `/dc team leave`, `/dc team kick <member>`, `/dc team transfer <member>` and `/dc team disband`. Each one asks you to confirm within 60 seconds.

Team changes are refused while someone is carrying a block or a creature with Carry On. Put it down first. If a change is refused for another reason, read the message and ask the server owner. Don't try to get around it by editing player data.
