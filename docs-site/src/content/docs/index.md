This guide covers **1.1.0 Beta** of the Dimensional Chaos mod and OneBlock modpack for Minecraft 1.21.1 with NeoForge. Older worlds can keep settings and OneBlock pools from the version that created them.

The [mod](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/dimensional-chaos) provides the OneBlock, island borders, gateways and escape mechanics. The [OneBlock modpack](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/modpacks/dimensional-chaos-oneblock) adds the other mods and pack settings used for the full survival progression. Quests and teams use supported Slate integrations; extra dimensions need their own mods.
