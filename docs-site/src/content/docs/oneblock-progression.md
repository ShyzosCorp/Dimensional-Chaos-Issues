The OneBlock changes what it gives as you progress. It has seven phases. Each one starts when you've mined enough blocks **or** when your island reaches its tier, whichever comes first. A quest chapter never has to wait for you to mine more.

| Phase | Blocks mined | or island tier |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Beginnings | 0 | – |
| Survival | 128 | 1 |
| Engineering | 512 | 3 |
| Arcane | 1,536 | 4 |
| Industry | 4,096 | 5 |
| Dimensional Chaos | 8,192 | 6 |
| Advanced Survival | 16,384 | 7 |

Later phases keep the key ores of earlier ones, so you won't run out of something like osmium.

Use `/dc oneblock info` to see where you are. A team shares one OneBlock and one counter. Worlds created before 1.1.2 keep the phases they were created with.

Mine it yourself, in Survival, with a tool that can harvest the current block. Machines and fake players don't advance it.

If your inventory is full, drops go into the island's six-slot reserve. Sneak and right-click the OneBlock with an empty hand to open it, or use `/dc oneblock cache`. Your team shares it. When both are full, drops fall on the ground, so empty it now and then.

The phases are separate from the eight [island expansion tiers](../island-expansion/), even though tiers can start a phase early.
