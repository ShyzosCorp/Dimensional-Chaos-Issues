The [mod](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/dimensional-chaos) brings the OneBlock, island borders, the gateway and the escape mechanics. The [OneBlock modpack](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/modpacks/dimensional-chaos-oneblock) adds the other mods, the quest book and the pack settings for the full survival experience.

Playing with friends? Have a look at [Multiplayer](./multiplayer/) before joining a team. Something wrong? Try [Troubleshooting](./troubleshooting/) or [open an issue](https://github.com/ShyzosCorp/Dimensional-Chaos-Issues/issues/new/choose).

These guides are up to date for **1.1.2 Beta**. Worlds created with an older version keep some of their old settings, like their OneBlock phases.
