A new island starts with a square border at radius 16. The turquoise wall shows up as you get close to the edge, and the server enforces it: you can't build or walk past it.

## Unlocking a tier

Each tier has an expansion chapter in the Slate quest book. Its last quest, **Island Expansion 1** to **8**, opens once the chapter's key quests are done. Then tick **Expand the island**. There's no item cost. The other quests in the chapter are optional.

Tiers unlock in order. The border grows by 8 blocks of radius each time, up to 80 at tier 8. The tier belongs to the island, so your whole team shares it, and it never goes down. `/dc island` shows your tier and the next one.

| Tier | Chapter | Border radius | Key quests |
| ---: | --- | ---: | --- |
| 1 | First Steps | 24 | First logs, stone tools, furnace, bridge to the gateway, first trip, back home in time |
| 2 | Survivor's Island | 32 | Iron, iron pickaxe, shield, visit the Twilight Forest |
| 3 | Mystical Roots | 40 | The Nether, quartz, inferium, obsidian |
| 4 | Gears & Steam | 48 | Zinc, andesite alloy, cogwheels, water wheel, mechanical press |
| 5 | Arcane Studies | 56 | The Undergarden, novice spell book, inscription table, lapis |
| 6 | Industrial Void | 64 | The Mining World, osmium, metallurgic infuser, steel, basic circuits, enrichment chamber |
| 7 | Beyond the Fracture | 72 | The End, ender pearls, wither skeletons |
| 8 | Dimensional Mastery | 80 | The Wither, nether star, beacon, elite circuit |

Each expansion reward is a kit for the next chapter: andesite alloy before Create, torches and lapis before magic, osmium and redstone before Mekanism, golden apples and ender pearls before the End, and the wither skulls and soul sand for the Wither. You also get XP and Dimensional Fragments.

Expanding gives you room to build. It doesn't fill the new area. It can also start a [OneBlock phase](../oneblock-progression/) early.

## New dimensions

Your tier decides which dimensions the gateway can take you to:

| Dimension | Island tier |
| --- | ---: |
| Twilight Forest | 0 |
| The Aether | 1 |
| The Nether | 2 |
| The Undergarden | 4 |
| Allthemodium Mining World | 5 |
| The End | 6 |
| Allthemodium The Other | 6 |
| The Otherside (Deeper and Darker) | 7 |

Unlocking a dimension adds it to your gateway's rotation; you can't pick it on demand. When a chapter needs a dimension, the gateway goes there first, at most every other day. A dimension only shows up if its mod is installed. Servers with custom settings may differ.

A higher tier also makes things harder: tougher Daily Threat waves, more fragments to repair a fracture, and bigger Rift Guardian waves.

## Without Slate Quests

The border and tiers come from the mod, but the quest unlocks need Slate Quests and the modpack's quest book. Without them, only an operator can change a tier, with `/dc island tier set <tier>`.
